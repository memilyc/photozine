✂️ Zine Assembly Guide

  • Maximized Print Space: Zero gap design. Exports directly using physical coordinates to protect high-definition photo assets from memory crashes.
  • Top Row (7, 6, 5, 4) images face upside down naturally to balance out properly once folded.
  • Cut purely along the center red dashed line dividing the top and bottom rows.
